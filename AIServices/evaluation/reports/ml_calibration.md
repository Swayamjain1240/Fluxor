## Limitations

- Ground truth is synthetic injection, not confirmed astronomical anomalies.
- Selection used 8 validation objects (171 positives); the chosen operating point is therefore itself uncertain, which is why validation F1 across the whole grid is reported.
- A per-curve flag fraction forces a constant number of flags per light curve, which is a strong assumption when real anomaly rates vary between objects.
- The pooled threshold is a single global cut; it ignores per-object score distributions, which is exactly what the per-curve strategy exploits.
- Metrics remain conditional on the injected morphologies (flare, dip, sinusoid, rectangular offset) and one TESS sector per object.
