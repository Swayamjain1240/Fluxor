## Limitations

- Ground truth is synthetic injection, not confirmed astronomical anomalies.
- Injected morphologies are parametric (flare, dip, sinusoid, rectangular offset) and do not cover blended binaries, detector glitches or granulation.
- contamination='auto' sets a fixed decision threshold (offset_ = -0.5) rather than a target quantile, so the flagged fraction is data-dependent: measured 11.6% on the pooled training fit but 0.8% on held-out test objects. Semantics B's low recall is a threshold-calibration failure, not a ranking failure, since its ROC-AUC is 0.9265.
- anomalyScore is a saturating transform of the decision function and is not a calibrated probability.
- Windows overlap by 50%, so window counts overstate the number of independent observations; bootstrap intervals therefore resample objects.
- Semantics A fits on the evaluated object's own windows; its scores are optimistic relative to cross-object generalisation and are reported only because they match deployed behaviour.
- Only one sector per object is used, so multi-sector behaviour is out of scope.
