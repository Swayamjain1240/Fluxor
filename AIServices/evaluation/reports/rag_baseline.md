## Limitations

- Ground truth is a single-annotator judgment set located with anchor-assisted search, so no inter-annotator agreement can be reported.
- Passages outside the judged candidate pool are treated as irrelevant; a retriever that surfaces a genuinely relevant unjudged passage is penalised.
- Ranking is exact cosine over production embeddings; Chroma's ANN index and persistence are not exercised, so this measures retrieval quality rather than index latency.
- The corpus is 12 documents; recall is bounded by a small gold set of at most five spans per query, so small metric differences are within annotation noise.
- Only retrieval quality is measured. No LLM answer quality, faithfulness or citation correctness is claimed.
